RMC-004
Measure of the Wind off Bramford Moor, n.d.
Digital composite: reworked installation photograph, layered surface scans, erasure
After cast concrete, oxidized steel rebar, found wire (casting lost)
Image 1024 × 1024 px; object approx. 60 × 40 × 50 cm

A static impression of transient kinetic force. The physical casting was lost, and the only installation photograph has since been reworked. Scans of the concrete surface are layered over the room, the plinth has been replaced by an empty fill, and the areas where the image failed to reconstruct have been left visible.

The record no longer documents the object; it documents its own repair. Neither the room nor the plinth can be confirmed to have existed. Further edits are permitted under open fabrication terms.
