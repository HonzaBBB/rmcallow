Measure of the Wind off Bramford Moor, n.d.
Cast concrete, oxidized steel rebar, found wire (documented as digital composite)

A static impression of transient kinetic force. Following the loss of the physical casting, the work survives solely as an uncalibrated installation composite and an open fabrication note.
